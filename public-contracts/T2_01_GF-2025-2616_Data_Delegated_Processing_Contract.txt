VII . Glossary of Terms :
( a ) Raw data: refers to data that is generated for the first time or collected at the source and has not been processed.
(ii) Result data: refers to data generated in the form of final product services during data processing activities to achieve the purpose of contract delivery.
(iii) Process data: This refers to data generated during the data processing activities of contract performance, which is different from the result data. It is generally a byproduct of the contract performance process.
( iv ) Data entrustment: refers to the entrustment of data rights by the data entrustment party to the entrusted party, and the entrusted party carrying out data processing activities in accordance with the purpose, method and scope agreed with the entrustment party.
( v ) Data holding rights: refers to the right of the right holder to hold legally acquired data, either by themselves or by entrusting others to hold it on their behalf. This aims to prevent others from illegally or improperly stealing, altering, disclosing, or destroying the data held by the right holder.
( vi ) Data usage rights: These refer to the rights holder's right to use data for optimizing production and operation, providing social services, or creating derivative data through processing, aggregation, analysis, etc. Generally speaking, usage rights are the rights holder's right to use data internally without distributing it to external parties.
( vii ) Data management rights: refers to the right of the right holder to provide data to external parties through paid or unpaid means such as transfer, licensing, investment or establishment of security.
