In accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China, the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Regulations on the Administration of Network Data Security, and other relevant laws and regulations, in order to safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of the contracting parties, promote data sharing and utilization, facilitate the orderly flow of data, and make it convenient for the contracting parties to use the data, the following explanations are provided:
I. This contract template is a recommended contract for reference only when data providers and data receivers enter into contracts .
II . Fourth , both parties shall carefully and comprehensively verify the transaction parties before the transaction. If a party is a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity, the contract shall be signed by the guardian.
V. In the event of any discrepancy between the handwritten and printed text in this contract and its supplementary clauses, supplementary agreements, and attachments, the handwritten text shall prevail. Similarly , in the event of any discrepancy between Arabic numerals and capitalized numerals, the capitalized numerals shall prevail.
VI . Matters not covered in this contract may be agreed upon separately by the parties in an appendix.
VII . Glossary of Terms:
( a ) Target data: refers to the target data provided by the data provider to the data recipient in this data provision activity .
(ii) Data provision: refers to the act of a data provider supplying data to a data recipient through paid or unpaid means such as transfer, licensing (including sharing) .
( iii ) Derivative data: refers to data that data processors have the right to use, which, under the premise of protecting the legitimate rights and interests of all parties, achieve substantial changes in the content, form, and structure of the data through the use of professional knowledge processing, modeling analysis, key information extraction, etc., thereby significantly enhancing the value of the data.
( iv ) Data property rights : refers to the property rights enjoyed by the right holder over specific data, including data ownership rights, data usage rights, data management rights, etc.
( v ) Data holding rights: refers to the right of the right holder to hold legally acquired data, either by themselves or by entrusting others to hold it on their behalf. This aims to prevent others from illegally or improperly stealing, altering, disclosing, or destroying the data held by the right holder.
( vi ) Data usage rights: These refer to the rights holder's right to use data for optimizing production and operation, providing social services, or creating derivative data through processing, aggregation, analysis, etc. Generally speaking, usage rights are the rights holder's right to use data internally without distributing it to external parties.
( vii ) Data management rights: refers to the right of the right holder to provide data to external parties through paid or unpaid means such as transfer, licensing, investment or establishment of security.
