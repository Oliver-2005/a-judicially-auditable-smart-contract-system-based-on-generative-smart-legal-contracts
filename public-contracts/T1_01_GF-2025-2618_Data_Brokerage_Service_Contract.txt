In accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China, the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Regulations on the Administration of Network Data Security, and other relevant laws and regulations, in order to safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of the contracting parties, promote data sharing and utilization, facilitate the orderly flow of data, and make it convenient for the contracting parties to use the data, the following explanations are provided:
I. This contract template is a recommended template and is intended for reference only when data clients and data intermediaries enter into contracts .
II . Before entering into this contract, both parties shall carefully read the entire contents of this contract and determine the optional, supplementary, fill-in, and modifyable contents based on the specific circumstances. Third , both parties may modify, supplement or delete the terms of this contract , but shall not violate the mandatory provisions of laws and administrative regulations, violate public order and good morals, or unfairly and unreasonably increase the buyer's liability, exclude or restrict the buyer's rights or reduce or exempt the seller's liability.
Fourth , both parties shall carefully and comprehensively verify the transaction parties before the transaction. If a party is a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity, the contract shall be signed by the guardian.
V. In the event of any discrepancy between the handwritten and printed text in this contract and its supplementary clauses, supplementary agreements, and attachments, the handwritten text shall prevail. Similarly , in the event of any discrepancy between Arabic numerals and capitalized numerals, the capitalized numerals shall prevail.
VI . Matters not covered in this contract may be separately agreed upon by the parties in an appendix, which shall form part of this contract.
VII . Glossary of Terms:
( a ) Subject data: refers to a series of data corresponding to the intermediary services stipulated in this contract.
( ii ) Data intermediary services: refers to the transaction matching activities provided by the intermediary to the client, such as market promotion, customer connection, and contract signing, in accordance with the contract.
( iii ) Data holding rights: refers to the right of the right holder to hold legally acquired data, either by themselves or by entrusting others to hold it on their behalf. This aims to prevent others from illegally or improperly stealing, altering, disclosing, or destroying the data held by the right holder.
( iv ) Data usage rights: These refer to the rights holder's right to use data for optimizing production and operation, providing social services, or creating derivative data through processing, aggregation, analysis, and other means. Generally speaking, usage rights are the rights holder's right to use data internally without distributing it to external parties.
( v ) Data management rights: refers to the right of the right holder to provide data to external parties through paid or unpaid means such as transfer, licensing, investment or establishment of security.

4 Rights of All Parties
1. Party A has the right to require Party B to provide data intermediary services in accordance with the contract .
2. Party A has the right to require Party B to report on the progress of the intermediary activities in a timely manner .
3. Party A has the right to require Party B to truthfully disclose information and circumstances related to the conclusion of the data transaction contract .
4. If Party A enters into a contract with a third party without utilizing the opportunity or intermediary services provided by Party B , Party A shall not be required to pay Party B an intermediary service fee.
5. Party B has the right to demand that Party A pay the data intermediary service fee in a timely and full manner as agreed .
6. Party B has the right to request Party A to provide necessary assistance, including providing relevant information or certificates related to the target data, so that Party B can conduct necessary reviews.
7. Party A confirms that it □ grants □ does not grant Party B the right to operate the data . If Party A grants Party B the right to operate the data, Party B shall acquire the right to operate the data upon signing this contract with Party A , and shall exercise the right to operate the data within the scope of authorization as required by Party A. Party A shall cooperate with Party B in exercising the right to operate the data and ensure that the data is provided to third parties as agreed.
5 Obligations of All Parties
1. If Party A uses the transaction opportunities or intermediary services provided by Party B to bypass Party B and directly enter into a data transaction contract, Party A is obligated to pay Party B the data intermediary service fee in accordance with the law .
2. Party A ensures that the source of the target data is legal, the ownership is clear, and the holding is compliant .
3. Party B shall provide data intermediary services in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations and the terms of this contract .
4. Party B shall provide Party A with timely, comprehensive, and accurate information on market conditions, transaction opportunities, and potential demanders.
5. If Party B provides intermediary services for concluding data transaction contracts, it shall provide Party A with relevant consultation and services for concluding contracts, and assist Party A in concluding contracts with third parties and handling relevant procedures.
6. Other obligations stipulated by law or this contract.
6 Data Security Requirements
All parties shall strictly handle the target data in accordance with the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China, the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Regulations on the Administration of Network Data Security, and other relevant laws and regulations.
