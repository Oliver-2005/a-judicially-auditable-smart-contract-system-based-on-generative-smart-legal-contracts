VII . Glossary of Terms :
( a ) Raw data: refers to data that is generated for the first time or collected at the source and has not been processed.
( ii ) Result data: refers to data generated in the form of final product services during data processing activities to achieve the purpose of contract delivery.
( iii ) Data fusion development: refers to the behavior of multiple entities collecting and integrating data from multiple sources to generate new data products or services with application value through business cooperation activities such as providing data products or services or carrying out business management.
( iv ) Data holding rights: refers to the right of the right holder to hold legally acquired data, either by themselves or by entrusting others to hold it on their behalf. This aims to prevent others from illegally or improperly stealing, altering, disclosing, or destroying the data held by the right holder.
( v ) Data usage rights: These refer to the rights holder's right to use data for optimizing production and operation, providing social services, or creating derivative data through processing, aggregation, analysis, and other means. Generally speaking, usage rights are the rights holder's right to use data internally without distributing it to external parties.
( vi ) Data management rights: refers to the right of the right holder to provide data to external parties through paid or unpaid means such as transfer, licensing, investment or establishment of security.
